# Prepared assessment data

`fuel_stations_processed.csv` is generated by `python manage.py prepare_fuel_data` from the supplied, unchanged root CSV. Prepared on 2026-10-04 using Census `Public_AR_Current` and the GeoNames US postal export retrieved that day.

- `census`: validated address-range/junction geocoding, not independently verified rooftop coordinates. Matches must retain the supplied city and meaningful road identifiers without invented street numbers.
- `city_centroid`: mean of unique postal coordinates for the exact normalized city/state. Only footprints with a maximum 10-mile radius qualify. These are approximate city locations, not fabricated station points.
- Unmatched rows are excluded from the app and retained in `fuel_stations_unmatched.csv` for review.

`preprocessing_stats.json` contains counts, percentages, source URL, benchmark, and SHA-256 hashes. Check the original CSV hash against `source_sha256`. The original CSV is not rewritten. Census responses and GeoNames downloads are cached locally in ignored `.cache/preprocessing/`.

`address_audit_before.json` and `address_audit_after.json` classify original address descriptions and cached Census outcomes before/after query normalization. Reproduce the current audit with `python manage.py audit_fuel_addresses`. Raw Census `Match` statuses are not acceptance counts: unsafe matches are explicitly rejected. Final data contains 154 Census records, 6,859 city centroids and 492 excluded records. Centroid coordinates and route progress are approximate; the optimizer uses separate city corridors, access uncertainty budgets and a selection penalty.
